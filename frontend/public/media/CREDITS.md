## How we changed the clips

- **Trims and crops.** Each clip is trimmed and cropped to 16:9 (and to 9:16 for phones).
- **Grade.** Every clip gets the same light grade: a little more contrast, slightly darker, cool shadows and warm mid-tones.
- **The hero.** It is the only clip that loops; it cross-fades its last second into its first.
- **Every other clip.** Each is scrubbed by the scroll, re-timed to 15 fps with a keyframe every 5 frames.

The originals are not in the repository.
